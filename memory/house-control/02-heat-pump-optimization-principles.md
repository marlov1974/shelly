# Heat Pump Optimization Principles

## Core design principle

The house has two heat pumps with partly different roles. They should not be treated as identical generic heat sources.

Future heat-pump control and optimization shall consider:

1. the current and forecasted energy need of the house,
2. the thermal storage status of the house,
3. current and forecasted energy prices,
4. the different roles and capabilities of the two heat pumps.

The optimizer should therefore decide heat-pump operation from system-level house needs and thermal-storage strategy, not only from instantaneous room temperature or simple on/off thresholds.
